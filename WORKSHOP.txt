[h1]Quick Call[/h1]
Calls to Wild and your suppliers open on their choices.

[h2]What's new in 1.0.1[/h2]
[list]
[*]If the game no longer has a method the mod hooks (after a game update, for example), the mod takes down what it had set up, writes one line to Player.log and stays off. Before, it could run half enabled.
[*]The rule for dialing at once is covered by a test that runs outside the game. Nothing changes in play.
[/list]

[h2]What it removes[/h2]
[list]
[*]The 2.25 s wait after the last digit: a contact's number dials as soon as it is complete and no other contact's number starts with the same digits.
[*]The 1-5 s of ringing and the pickup pause.
[*]The greeting lines: every call opens on the line with the choices, as in the game's own quick dial.
[*]The pause before the choices appear.
[/list]

[h2]What stays[/h2]
[list]
[*]Intro calls, cooldown replies and story calls play in full.
[*]Everything after you pick a choice works as before.
[/list]

Needs no special permissions. Built for game version 0.46D.

[h1]快速通话[/h1]
给王尔德和供货商打电话时，一接通就是对话选项。

[h2]1.0.1 更新内容[/h2]
[list]
[*]游戏更新后，如果模组挂接的某个方法不存在，模组会撤下已经装好的部分，在 Player.log 里写一行说明，然后保持关闭，不再只启用一半。
[*]立即拨号的规则现在有一个在游戏外运行的测试。游玩时没有变化。
[/list]

[h2]去掉了什么[/h2]
[list]
[*]输完最后一位数字后的 2.25 秒等待：号码输完整、且没有别的联系人号码以这几位开头时，立即拨出。
[*]1 到 5 秒的响铃和接通前的停顿。
[*]开场白：每次通话都从带选项的那句开始，和游戏自带的快捷拨号一样。
[*]选项出现前的停顿。
[/list]

[h2]保留了什么[/h2]
[list]
[*]初次介绍、冷却期回复和剧情电话照常完整播放。
[*]选完选项之后的一切都和原来一样。
[/list]

不需要任何特殊权限。适用于游戏版本 0.46D。
