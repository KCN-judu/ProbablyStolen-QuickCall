[h1]Quick Call[/h1]
Calling Wild or your suppliers? Skip the wait and get straight to business.

[h2]What it removes[/h2]
[list]
[*]The 2.25 s wait after the last digit: a contact's number dials as soon as you finish typing it.
[*]The 1-5 s of ringing and the pickup pause.
[*]The greeting lines: every call opens on the line with the choices, using the game's own quick-dial dialog.
[*]The pause before the choices appear.
[/list]

[h2]What stays[/h2]
[list]
[*]Intro calls, cooldown replies and story calls play in full.
[*]Everything after you pick a choice works as before.
[/list]

Needs no special permissions. Built for game version 0.46D.

[h1]快速通话[/h1]
给 Wild 或供货商打电话？跳过等待，直接谈正事。

[h2]去掉了什么[/h2]
[list]
[*]输完最后一位数字后的 2.25 秒等待：联系人号码一输完就立即拨出。
[*]1 到 5 秒的响铃和接通前的停顿。
[*]寒暄台词：每次通话都直接从带选项的那句开始，用的是游戏自带的快速拨号对话。
[*]选项出现前的停顿。
[/list]

[h2]保留了什么[/h2]
[list]
[*]初次介绍、冷却期回复和剧情电话照常完整播放。
[*]选完选项之后的一切都和原来一样。
[/list]

不需要任何特殊权限。适用于游戏版本 0.46D。
